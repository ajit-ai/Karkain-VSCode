# Support

- Bug reports and feature requests:
  [Karkain-VSCode issues](https://github.com/ajit-ai/Karkain-VSCode/issues).
  Include `karkain --version` output, the extension version, OS, and a minimal
  `.kark` reproducer.
- Karkain language/toolchain bugs belong to
  [Karkain issues](https://github.com/ajit-ai/Karkain/issues).
- No telemetry is collected. No credentials are handled. Do not post secrets.
